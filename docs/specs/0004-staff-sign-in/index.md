# 0004. Staff sign in with Better Auth, invite links, and the `staff` row

**Date**: 2026-09-12
**Status**: In Progress

**Revised 2026-09-19**: Clerk is replaced by Better Auth (an authentication library the app hosts itself, with its tables in the project's own Postgres). Every auth decision was reopened through `/architect`; the `ensure_staff()` mechanism and the three way `currentStaff()` result survived, everything Clerk specific did not. The acceptance criteria, design, and build plan below are the revised contract. See [rationale.md](rationale.md) for the reasoning, the options weighed, and a short history of the Clerk version. [verify.md](verify.md) still describes the Clerk build and must be regenerated (see Follow-up).

**Revised 2026-09-21**: sign in is by **username and password**, never by email, and Google sign in is removed. Every account has a username (3 to 30 lower case letters, digits, dots or underscores, fixed once chosen) through Better Auth's `username` plugin; the email column Better Auth requires holds a placeholder (`<username>@staff.invalid`) that nothing reads, and the gate refuses any other value. `staff.email` becomes `staff.username`, `staff_invite.claimed_email` becomes `claimed_username`, `BOOTSTRAP_OWNER_EMAIL` becomes `BOOTSTRAP_OWNER_USERNAME`, and the minted token carries `username` instead of `email`. Existing accounts were given a username from the local part of their email by migration `20260921032725_username_sign_in`. Where the text below still says email or Google, read it through this note; the acceptance criteria and the schema section are updated in place.

## Summary

Staff sign in with a username and password through Better Auth running inside the app, and nobody can create an account except by opening a one time link that an owner or superadmin made on the users screen. The moment a signed in person loads the board, a small database function creates or refreshes their `staff` row from a short lived token the server mints (signs) for Supabase, so every existing policy keeps working unchanged. The same one time links let the owner reset a forgotten password without an email service, and deactivating someone ends their sessions at once. Row level security policies (rules in Postgres that decide who may touch each row) still decide who may change the schedule; this feature is what gives them a row to read.

## Requirements

**User stories**:

- As a staff member, I want to sign in once on my phone and stay signed in through my shifts, so that keeping the schedule current takes no extra steps.
- As a staff member, I want to sign out when I hand a shared tablet to someone else, so that my name is not on their changes.
- As a staff member who forgot my password, I want Ella to be able to hand me a link that lets me set a new one, so that I am not locked out until an engineer runs SQL.
- As Ella (owner) or a superadmin, I want to make a link for each new hire myself and send it however I like, so that nobody I did not choose can hold an account and I never have to type or know anyone's password.
- As Ella or a superadmin, I want a leaver switched off and signed out everywhere in one step, so that a phone left in a bag cannot touch the schedule.
- As Winston (the engineer), I want the identity data in the project's own database with no vendor dashboard, so that the move to a self hosted container carries everything with it.
- As a player, I want the board to stay open with no sign in and no clutter, so that checking a court takes a second.

**Acceptance criteria** (the contract, each criterion is IDed and independently checkable):

- **AC-1**: Account creation is invite only. Opening `/sign-up/[token]` with a pending invite (not claimed, not revoked, not expired) shows a form for display name, username, and password; submitting creates a Better Auth user and claims the invite in the same step, so a second use of the same link is refused. Any attempt to create an account without a valid invite, whether through our page or a direct request to `/api/auth/sign-up/email` (including one with no username, or with an email other than the username's placeholder), is refused inside Better Auth's `user.create.before` database hook, and the person sees the fixed line "This board is for staff. Ask Ella for an invite link if you need one."
- **AC-2**: Bootstrap. While the `better_auth.user` table is empty, one account may be created with no invite, only when the username equals `BOOTSTRAP_OWNER_USERNAME` (compared lower cased and trimmed), through a bootstrap form at `/sign-up` that is shown only in that state. That person's `staff` row is `owner`. Any other username is refused as in AC-1, and once one user exists the exception is closed and `/sign-up` with no token shows the staff only line.
- **AC-3**: An active `owner` or `superadmin` can, on `/staff/admin/users`, make an invite link (choosing a role from `staff`, `admin`, `superadmin`; `superadmin` disabled once two rows hold it, `owner` never offered) or a reset link for one existing active account. The full link is shown once in a dialog with a Copy button and a note that it cannot be shown again. A pending links list under the staff table shows kind, role or target name, who made it, when it expires, and a Revoke button; claimed, revoked, and expired links do not appear. A link expires 7 days after creation. `staff` and `admin` cannot make, see, or revoke links.
- **AC-4**: Sign in at `/sign-in` with username and password (`authClient.signIn.username`). A username is 3 to 30 characters of lower case letters, digits, underscores and dots (a dot only between two runs of the others), typed in any case and compared lower cased, and cannot be changed once chosen. A session lasts 30 days, is refreshed daily while used, and survives a page refresh and a closed and reopened browser. Sign out returns to `/` with no staff controls showing.
- **AC-5**: On every page load by a signed in person, `ensure_staff()` creates or refreshes their `staff` row keyed by `user_id` (the `sub` of the minted token): `display_name` and `username` mirror the token's `name` and `username` (trimmed name, falling back to the username, cut to 80), and `last_signed_in_at` is set. On creation the role is: `owner` when the table is empty (under the existing advisory lock), else the role of the claimed invite whose `claimed_username` matches and whose `claimed_by` is still null (which is then set to this id), else `staff`. Sign in never changes an existing row's `role` or `is_active`, and the caller cannot pass either value in.
- **AC-6**: The bridge. `staffSupabase()` calls Supabase with a token minted by `mintStaffToken()` in `lib/supabase/staff-token.ts`: HS256, signed with `SUPABASE_JWT_SECRET`, claims `sub`, `role: "authenticated"`, `aud: "authenticated"`, `username`, `name`, `iat`, `exp` five minutes out, cached per request. Every existing policy, `security definer` function, and `changed_by` value works unchanged, and a booking written by a signed in person has `changed_by` equal to their Better Auth user id. A test asserts that no module other than `lib/supabase/staff-token.ts` reads `SUPABASE_JWT_SECRET`.
- **AC-7**: Opening `/reset/[token]` with a pending reset link shows the account's username (read only) and a new password field; submitting claims the link, sets the password through Better Auth, and deletes every session of that account, so the person signs in again with the new password. A claimed, revoked, or expired link shows a plain message and the staff only line.
- **AC-8**: When `updateStaffRole` sets `is_active = false`, it deletes every Better Auth session of that user after the row write succeeds, so that person's next request is signed out. Reactivating requires signing in again. A person whose row is inactive but somehow still holds a session (the five minute cookie cache) sees the deactivated notice and a sign out button, and every write is refused by policy as `forbidden`, exactly as before.
- **AC-9**: The name in the staff menu links to an account page at `/staff/account` (replacing Clerk's profile modal), open to every active staff member whatever their role. It shows username, role and the date the account was made (read only), lets the person edit their name (`authClient.updateUser`, then `router.refresh()` so `ensure_staff()` copies it onto the `staff` row), change their password (current password, new password, confirmation; success revokes every other session of the account), sign out every other device, and sign out this one (the Sign out button lives in the Details section here, not in the header; the header keeps a Sign out only for the switched off and could not load states, which cannot reach this page). Passwords are 10 to 128 characters.
- **AC-10**: A signed out visitor can change nothing. `proxy.ts` sends a request under `/staff` with no session cookie to `/sign-in?redirect=<path>` (the CSV route keeps answering its own 401), and `currentStaff()` still verifies the session on the page. Every Server Action returns `unauthenticated` before touching the database. The `better_auth` schema is not exposed to PostgREST and has no grant to `anon` or `authenticated`; `anon` cannot execute any invite function; `staff_invite` is readable only by an active `owner` or `superadmin` and its `token_hash` column is never granted to `authenticated`.
- **AC-11**: Clerk is gone: no `@clerk/*` package, no `NEXT_PUBLIC_CLERK_*` or `CLERK_*` variable, no Clerk import, `proxy.ts` no longer wraps `clerkMiddleware`, `supabase/config.toml` has `[auth.third_party.clerk] enabled = false`, and `.env.example` lists the new variables. In development with no `BETTER_AUTH_SECRET` the public board still boots; a production build refuses to serve without it.
- **AC-12**: The staff board's realtime listener joins the `schedule` topic through the same anon browser client the public board uses, with no `realtime.setAuth()` and no second client module, and still refetches through the session bound read on every broadcast.
- **AC-13**: Analytics identify staff by Better Auth user id. Four events join the `.strict()` allow list and fire only after a successful write, never awaited: `staff_invite_created` (kind, role), `staff_invite_revoked` (kind), `staff_invite_redeemed` (kind, method, always `password`), `staff_password_changed` (source `reset` or `account`).
- **AC-14**: Better Auth's rate limiter runs with `storage: "database"` (its counters survive serverless instances) and reads the client address from `x-forwarded-for`; its default sensitive endpoint rule (3 requests per 10 seconds on sign in, sign up, and change password) is kept. The existing public read limiter in `proxy.ts` is unchanged and never sees `/api/auth/*`.
- **AC-15**: Failure handling. If Better Auth cannot reach Postgres, `/sign-in` shows "Could not sign in right now" and the error is logged with `console.error`; the public board is unaffected. `currentStaff()` keeps its three way result and the three second abort. An invite claimed by a sign up that then failed is burnt (it no longer appears as pending) and the owner makes a new one; this is stated on the users screen's empty state text.
- **AC-16**: `/sign-in`, `/sign-up`, `/sign-up/[token]`, and `/reset/[token]` are the project's own forms (shadcn inputs and buttons, `react-hook-form` with the Zod schemas) inside `AppShell` with no toolbar, match the design tokens in both themes, are keyboard usable, meet WCAG AA contrast, are marked `noindex`, and redirect a signed in visitor to `/staff`.

## Decision

**Chosen option**: Bridge Option 1 (mint a short lived Supabase JWT per request) with accounts Option A (owner generated one time links), the gate inside Better Auth's `user.create.before` hook.

Better Auth owns identity and sessions in a `better_auth` schema of the project's own Postgres, reached through a dedicated `better_auth_app` role over the Supavisor pooler; the app mints a five minute Supabase token from the confirmed session so every existing policy keeps reading `auth.jwt() ->> 'sub'`; accounts exist only through single use, seven day links the owner makes on the users screen, which also serve as password reset links.

## Feature design

**Data model sketch**:

`better_auth` schema, generated by `npx @better-auth/cli generate` from `lib/auth.ts` and checked in as one migration. Owned by the `better_auth_app` role; no grant of any kind to `anon`, `authenticated`, or `public`; not in PostgREST's exposed schemas. Column names are whatever the CLI generates (camel case, quoted); nothing of ours reads them except by user id through Better Auth's own adapter.

| Table          | Key columns                                                                                         | Note                                                                      |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `user`         | `id text pk`, `name`, `username unique`, `email unique` (placeholder), `emailVerified`, `image`, `createdAt`, `updatedAt` | identity only; role lives on `public.staff`; `username` is the `username` plugin's column |
| `session`      | `id pk`, `userId fk → user`, `token unique`, `expiresAt`, `ipAddress`, `userAgent`                  | 30 day life, daily refresh                                                |
| `account`      | `id pk`, `userId fk → user`, `providerId`, `accountId`, `password`, OAuth token columns (unused)    | one row per user: `credential` (password)                                  |
| `verification` | `id pk`, `identifier`, `value`, `expiresAt`                                                         | Better Auth internal (OAuth state)                                        |
| `rateLimit`    | `key`, `count`, `lastRequest`                                                                       | present because `rateLimit.storage` is `database`                          |

| Column                                                                        | Type            | Change      | Note                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `user_id`                                                                     | `text pk`       | **renamed** | from `clerk_user_id`; equals `better_auth.user.id` and the `sub` of the minted token. `public.staff_invite` (new)

| Column           | Type                                                                | Note                                                                                                        |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `id`             | `uuid pk default gen_random_uuid()`                                 |                                                                                                             |
| `token_hash`     | `text not null unique`                                              | `encode(sha256(token), 'hex')` of a 32 byte random token; the plain token is in the link only, shown once   |
| `kind`           | `text not null check (kind in ('invite', 'reset'))`                 |                                                                                                             |
| `role`           | `text check (role in ('staff', 'admin', 'superadmin'))`             | required when `kind = 'invite'`, null for a reset; `owner` moves only through the spec 0012 transfer         |
| `target_user_id` | `text references public.staff (user_id)`                            | required when `kind = 'reset'`, null for an invite                                                          |
| `expires_at`     | `timestamptz not null`                                              | `created_at + interval '7 days'`, set inside `create_staff_invite()`                                         |
| `created_by`     | `text not null references public.staff (user_id)`                   | the owner or superadmin who made it                                                                         |
| `created_at`     | `timestamptz not null default now()`                                |                                                                                                             |
| `claimed_at`     | `timestamptz`                                                       | set atomically by `claim_staff_invite()` (invites) or `claim_staff_reset()` (resets); single use             |
| `claimed_username` | `text`                                                            | the username of the account being created, so `ensure_staff()` can hand the invite's role to the right new row |
| `claimed_by`     | `text`                                                              | the `user_id` once the staff row exists (invites) or the target (resets)                                     |
| `revoked_at`     | `timestamptz`                                                       | set by `revoke_staff_invite()`                                                                               |

Constraints: `check ((kind = 'invite' and role is not null and target_user_id is null) or (kind = 'reset' and role is null and target_user_id is not null))`; partial index `staff_invite_pending_idx on (created_at desc) where claimed_at is null and revoked_at is null`. Rows are kept forever. `enable row level security`; `grant select (id, kind, role, target_user_id, expires_at, created_by, created_at, claimed_at, claimed_by, revoked_at) on public.staff_invite to authenticated` (column list on purpose: never `token_hash`); policy `"owner or superadmin may read invite links"` for select using `private.can_manage_staff()` (a new helper: caller's row is active and `role in ('owner', 'superadmin')`, the same test `update_staff_role()` inlines today). No insert, update, or delete grant to anyone; the functions below, `security definer`, are the only writers.

Functions (all `language plpgsql`, `security definer`, `set search_path = ''`, `revoke execute from public, anon`):

- `public.create_staff_invite(p_kind text, p_role text, p_target_user_id text, p_token_hash text) returns public.staff_invite`: raises `42501` unless `private.can_manage_staff()`; for a reset, raises `22023` unless the target row exists and is active and is not the caller; for an invite with `p_role = 'superadmin'`, raises `22023` when two rows already hold it (the same cap spec 0012 keeps in the screen, now also here since this is a second door); inserts with `created_by = auth.jwt() ->> 'sub'` and `expires_at = now() + interval '7 days'`. Grant execute to `authenticated`.
- `public.revoke_staff_invite(p_id uuid) returns void`: raises `42501` unless `private.can_manage_staff()`; `update ... set revoked_at = now() where id = p_id and claimed_at is null and revoked_at is null`; zero rows raises `P0002`. Grant execute to `authenticated`.
- `public.claim_staff_invite(p_token_hash text, p_username text) returns text`: `update public.staff_invite set claimed_at = now(), claimed_username = lower(btrim(p_username)) where token_hash = p_token_hash and kind = 'invite' and claimed_at is null and revoked_at is null and expires_at > now() returning role`; returns the role or null. Grant execute to `better_auth_app` only; this is the one function the auth layer may call, from the `user.create.before` hook, and the only thing `better_auth_app` can reach in `public` (`grant usage on schema public to better_auth_app`, nothing else).
- `public.claim_staff_reset(p_token_hash text) returns text`: same claim shape for `kind = 'reset'`, sets `claimed_by = target_user_id`, returns `target_user_id` or null. Grant execute to `better_auth_app` only; called by the reset Server Action through the auth pool, never with a staff token, because the person resetting has no session.
- `public.peek_staff_invite(p_token_hash text) returns table (kind text, target_username text)`: read only; returns the kind and, for a reset, the target's username, only for a pending link; used by the two link pages to render before anything is claimed. Grant execute to `better_auth_app` only, called through the auth pool for the same reason.
- `public.ensure_staff()`: unchanged signature and grants; the creation branch becomes `role = case when not exists (select 1 from public.staff) then 'owner' else coalesce((select i.role from public.staff_invite i where i.kind = 'invite' and i.claimed_username = v_username and i.claimed_by is null order by i.claimed_at desc limit 1), 'staff') end`, followed by `update public.staff_invite set claimed_by = v_sub where ... that same row`. The error message for a token with no name or username now names `mintStaffToken()` instead of the Clerk dashboard.

`private.can_manage_staff()` is added beside `private.is_owner()` and `private.is_active_staff()`, with the same grants. `update_staff_role()` keeps its inlined check (no behaviour change; a later cleanup may point it at the helper).

**State transitions**:

`staff_invite`: pending → claimed (`claim_staff_invite` or `claim_staff_reset`, once) · pending → revoked (`revoke_staff_invite`) · pending → expired (`expires_at` passes; no row change, the predicates exclude it). Claimed invite: `claimed_by` null → set (by `ensure_staff` on the new person's first page load). Nothing moves a link back to pending. A claimed invite whose account was never created (the user insert failed after the claim, or the pool response was lost) stays claimed with `claimed_by` null forever: accepted litter, invisible to the pending list, harmless because `ensure_staff` takes the newest claim for a username, and the owner simply makes a new link.

`staff` row: absent → active `owner` (first row ever) or the invite's role or `staff` (first page load) → inactive (`update_staff_role`, which also deletes sessions) → active again (`update_staff_role`, then a fresh sign in). Unchanged otherwise from spec 0012.

`better_auth.session`: created at sign in or invite redemption → refreshed daily → gone at expiry, sign out, deactivation, password reset (all sessions), or password change (all other sessions).

**Key invariants**:

1. No account exists without a claimed invite, except the single bootstrap account. Enforced in `databaseHooks.user.create.before`, which every creation path passes through; `emailAndPassword.disableSignUp` stays `false` because the hook is the gate and `disableSignUp` would also block our own server call.
2. A link is claimed at most once. Enforced by the `where claimed_at is null ... returning` update inside `claim_staff_invite()` and `claim_staff_reset()`; two concurrent redemptions cannot both get a row.
3. `token_hash` never leaves Postgres. It has no grant to `authenticated`, and the plain token exists only in the URL returned once by `createStaffInvite` and in the short lived signed cookie.
4. A `staff` row's `user_id` always equals the `sub` of the token that created it, and that `sub` is a Better Auth user id, because `mintStaffToken()` is the only issuer of tokens `staffSupabase()` uses and it copies `sub` from the confirmed session. There is no other insert path into `staff`.
5. Exactly one row is ever created with `role = 'owner'` by the bootstrap rule, under the existing advisory lock; the bootstrap account is the only account that can exist when the table is empty, so the two bootstraps agree.
6. `SUPABASE_JWT_SECRET` is read by `lib/supabase/staff-token.ts` and nowhere else. Enforced by a test in `lib/import-boundaries.test.ts` that greps `app/` and `lib/` for the name. Every minted token carries `role: "authenticated"` and a five minute `exp`; the module exports no way to mint anything else.
7. `better_auth_app` can reach exactly three functions in `public` (`claim_staff_invite`, `claim_staff_reset`, `peek_staff_invite`) and no table. `anon` and `authenticated` can reach nothing in `better_auth`. In app code, `authPool` (`lib/auth/pool.ts`) is the only handle on that role, and only `lib/auth.ts`, `lib/auth/actions.ts`, and the three public auth pages may import it; `lib/import-boundaries.test.ts` fails on any other importer, so no Server Action or query module can quietly bypass `staffSupabase()`.
7a. Two Server Actions run before any session exists (`redeemInvite`, `resetPassword`) and therefore do not call `requireStaff()`. This is a deliberate, named exception to the project rule; their gate is a link claimed atomically in Postgres, and they live together in `lib/auth/actions.ts` so the exception is visible in one file.
8. Sign in never changes `role` or `is_active`. Unchanged.
9. A page render never waits on `ensure_staff`. Unchanged.
10. Every timestamp is `timestamptz` in UTC; the expiry shown on the users screen is formatted in `Asia/Manila` by the existing helpers.

**Security model**:

- Anonymous: the public board, `/sign-in`, `/sign-up`, the two link pages, and `/api/auth/*`. No access to `staff`, `staff_invite`, or `better_auth`. Cannot create an account (the hook), cannot execute any invite function.
- Signed in `staff` or `admin`: everything spec 0012 gives them; no invite function, no pending list (the select policy refuses).
- `owner` and `superadmin`: make and revoke links, see the pending list, deactivate with session revocation. Cannot make a reset link for themselves (the function refuses `target = caller`), so nobody resets their own password through the owner path; they use the account page.
- Better Auth: CSRF and origin checks on (defaults), `trustedOrigins` is `[BETTER_AUTH_URL]`, `useSecureCookies` in production, rate limiting on with database storage, `scrypt` hashing, `advanced.ipAddress.ipAddressHeaders: ["x-forwarded-for"]`.
- The `better_auth_app` role: `login`, no `bypassrls`, owner of the `better_auth` schema and its tables, `usage` on `public`, execute on the three functions above, nothing else. Its password is set by hand once and lives only in `BETTER_AUTH_DATABASE_URL`.
- Personal data: `better_auth.user` holds name and username (and a placeholder email), a copy of what `staff` already holds; `session` holds IP address and user agent for 30 days. Spec 0010's privacy notice gains one sentence for the session data (Follow-up). `staff_invite` holds no personal data beyond `claimed_username`.
- `proxy.ts` is a courtesy door, not the lock: enforcement stays in `requireStaff()` and row level security.

- `SUPABASE_JWT_SECRET`: the project's legacy JWT secret from the dashboard's JWT settings. Prerequisite: leave the legacy HS256 key active; do not revoke it when rotating to asymmetric keys.
- `BOOTSTRAP_OWNER_USERNAME`: the one username allowed to create the first account.
- Removed: `NEXT_PUBLIC_CLERK_PUBLISHABLE_KEY`, `CLERK_SECRET_KEY`, the four `NEXT_PUBLIC_CLERK_*_URL` values. `lib/env.ts` gains a `serverEnv()` schema for the new values, validated lazily like `publicEnv()`.

## Consequences

**Positive**:

- Identity, sessions, and the audit of who invited whom all live in the project's own Postgres, so the Docker move carries them with it and no vendor dashboard is part of the setup.
- Nothing below `lib/supabase/staff.ts` changes: six migrations of policies and functions, the query modules, and the generated types stay as they are.
- Account creation is controlled at the one point every path passes through, and it works with no email service, which the venue does not want to run.
- Deactivation now ends sessions; a lost phone is out within minutes, not a month.
- Password reset exists without email, mediated by the person staff already talk to.

**Negative / tradeoffs**:

- `SUPABASE_JWT_SECRET` is in the app's environment and can sign a `service_role` token. The rule "no key in app code can bypass a policy" is now held by one module and one test instead of being impossible. This is the biggest cost of choosing a hosted library over the platform's own auth, and the reason the direct Postgres bridge stays on the list (Follow-up).
- The minted token depends on Supabase's legacy HS256 secret staying active. Revoking it in a future key rotation silently breaks every staff write until the app is updated.
- One more database role with a password set by hand, one more connection pool, and Better Auth's own tables to back up and migrate. Better Auth's CLI generates schema changes on upgrades that must be turned into forward only migrations by hand.
- Links are bearer tickets bound to nothing but a role; whoever opens one first gets the account. Accepted for seven day, single use links handed to one person by the owner.
- A forgotten password waits for an owner to make a link; there is no self service until an email service exists.
- The auth pages are our own forms now, so every state (wrong password, expired link, Postgres down) is ours to render and test; Clerk's cards did that for free.
- Two Zod boundaries and four new Postgres functions is more surface than a dashboard checkbox. The functions are small and each has a database test.

**Neutral**:

- Spec 0001's choice of Clerk and its rules 4, 10, 11, and 12 are revised by this spec; spec 0012's `clerkUserId` naming is renamed by build step 3 and 9. Both get a one line note when the scope is next reworked (Follow-up).
- The staff realtime listener loses its token, which it never needed: the `schedule` topic has been readable by `anon` since spec 0002 and carries no personal data. `lib/supabase/AGENTS.md` and spec 0005 invariant 6 describe a second client that no longer exists.
- Root `AGENTS.md` rules that mention Clerk (the stack line, "This is Next.js 16 and Clerk 7", the service role key rule's new caveat, the `server-only` list gaining `lib/auth.ts` and `lib/auth/pool.ts`) are `/sync`'s to update after the build, as is the "every Server Action calls `requireStaff()` first" rule, which gains the one named exception for the three pre authentication actions in `lib/auth/actions.ts`.
- `verify.md` in this spec directory describes the Clerk build and is stale until regenerated.

## Migration plan

**Strategy**: big bang, one deployment, justified because there are no real users and every row is test data; a strangler would mean running two identity systems against one `staff` table for nobody.
**Phases**:

1. Build and prove on the development project and `localhost` (build plan steps 1 to 12). The Clerk development instance keeps working on `main` until the branch merges.
2. Merge, set the new environment on Vercel, run `npx supabase db push` against the linked project, set the `better_auth_app` password, remove Clerk from the Supabase dashboard, deploy. Winston creates the bootstrap account, makes Ella's invite, and both prove a booking write.
   **Rollback**: revert the merge commit and restore the previous environment; migrations are forward only, so the database rollback is a new migration that recreates `clerk_user_id` (a rename back) and drops `staff_invite`, `better_auth`, and the role. Test data is truncated in step 3 either way, so nothing is lost by rolling back.
   **Risks**: the legacy JWT secret revoked or wrong (every staff write fails with a 401 from PostgREST; the fix is the env value); the pooler URL rejecting `better_auth_app` (password not set); a Better Auth upgrade regenerating schema.
